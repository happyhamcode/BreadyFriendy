Basic White Bread

A simple yet perfect recipe using just flour, water, salt, and yeast. Ideal for everyday loaves with a light texture.

  • Hydration: 60-65%
  • Ingredients: Flour, water, salt, yeast
  • Characteristics: Simple, straightforward recipe

Enriched Dough (Brioche)

Rich, tender dough with eggs and butter for exceptional softness and flavor. Perfect for enriched breads.

  • Hydration: 55-65% (effective hydration includes water from enriching ingredients)
  • Ingredients: Flour, water, salt, yeast, eggs, milk, butter
  • Characteristics: Rich, tender crumb, higher fat content

Sweet Dough (Challah)

Traditional braided bread using eggs and oil for distinctive flavor and texture. Sweet but not overly so.

  • Hydration: 55-65%
  • Ingredients: Flour, water, salt, yeast, eggs, oil
  • Characteristics: Sweet, tender, rich texture with braided appearance

Whole Wheat Bread

Nutritious dough using whole wheat flour with optional enrichments. Higher fiber content and robust flavor.

  • Hydration: 70-75% (higher due to whole wheat absorption)
  • Ingredients: Whole wheat flour, water, salt, yeast, optional enrichments
  • Characteristics: Nutrient-dense, denser crumb than white flour bread

Rye Bread

Distinctive tangy flavor using rye flour with possible enrichments. Higher hydration due to rye's absorption properties.

  • Hydration: 75-80% (higher due to rye's absorption)
  • Ingredients: Rye flour, water, salt, yeast, optional enrichments
  • Characteristics: Distinctive tangy flavor, dense texture

Italian Bread (Ciabatta, Pullman)

Light, airy crumb with crispy exterior. Great for sandwiches and traditional Italian breads.

  • Hydration: 60-70%
  • Ingredients: Flour, water, salt, yeast, optional enrichments
  • Characteristics: Light, airy crumb with a crispy exterior

Milk Bread

Soft, sweet, tender crumb with golden color from milk. Classic enriched bread.

  • Hydration: 65-70%
  • Ingredients: Flour, water, salt, yeast, milk, butter
  • Characteristics: Soft, sweet, tender crumb with golden color

Sourdough Bread

Fermented dough using a starter for complex flavor and texture. Develops unique taste over time.

  • Hydration: 65-75%
  • Ingredients: Flour, water, salt, sourdough starter
  • Characteristics: Tangy flavor, chewy texture, complex taste development

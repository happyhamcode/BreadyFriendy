# Dough Type Ingredient Analysis

## Basic Bread
- **Essential**: Flour, water, salt, yeast
- **Optional**: None typical
- **Characteristics**: Simple, straightforward recipe

## Enriched Dough (Brioche, Challah)
- **Essential**: Flour, water, salt, yeast
- **Enriching Ingredients**: Eggs, milk, butter
- **Characteristics**: Rich, tender crumb, higher fat content
- **Typical ratios**: 
  - Eggs: ~50-100g for 500g flour
  - Milk: ~50-100g for 500g flour  
  - Butter: ~20-40g for 500g flour

## Sweet Dough (Brioche, Cinnamon Rolls, Parker House Rolls)
- **Essential**: Flour, water, salt, yeast
- **Enriching Ingredients**: Eggs, milk, butter, sugar
- **Fat Ingredients**: Oil or additional butter
- **Characteristics**: Sweet, tender, rich texture

## Whole Wheat Bread
- **Essential**: Whole wheat flour, water, salt, yeast
- **Enriching Ingredients**: May include eggs, milk, butter (optional)
- **Characteristics**: Nutrient-dense, denser crumb than white flour bread

## Rye Bread
- **Essential**: Rye flour, water, salt, yeast
- **Enriching Ingredients**: May include eggs, milk, butter (optional)
- **Characteristics**: Distinctive tangy flavor, dense texture, often with sourdough starter

## Italian Bread
- **Essential**: Flour, water, salt, yeast
- **Enriching Ingredients**: May include eggs, milk, butter, olive oil or herbs
- **Characteristics**: Crusty exterior, tender interior, often uses a poolish or biga starter

## Recommendations for Ingredient Display

Based on typical recipes:
1. **Basic Bread** - Flour, water, salt, yeast only
2. **Enriched Dough** - Flour, water, salt, yeast, eggs, milk, butter  
3. **Sweet Dough** - Flour, water, salt, yeast, eggs, milk, butter, oil/sugar
4. **Whole Wheat Bread** - Flour, water, salt, yeast, optional eggs/milk/butter
5. **Rye Bread** - Rye flour, water, salt, yeast, optional eggs/milk/butter
6. **Italian Bread** - Flour, water, salt, yeast, optional eggs/milk/butter/olive oil

The key insight is that the enriching ingredients (eggs, milk, butter) provide both water and fat, while oil/sugar are purely fat ingredients that contribute to texture, not hydration.

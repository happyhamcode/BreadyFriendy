Measuring: Why Grams Beat Cups

A cup measures volume, and flour packs. The same cup of flour can weigh quite different amounts depending on how it was scooped.

How much does a cup of flour weigh?

Published figures differ:

SourceFlourGrams per cup
King ArthurAll-purpose120
King ArthurWhole wheat113
USDAAll-purpose125
USDAWhole grain120
USDABread flour137

The spread from 113 to 137 g is about 20%.

What that does to a recipe

A recipe says 4 cups of flour and 1½ cups of water. Water is 355 g. If the flour is 120 g a cup, that is 480 g and the hydration is 355 ÷ 480 = 74%, a sticky, open dough. If the flour is 137 g a cup, that is 548 g and the hydration is 65%, a soft sandwich dough. Same recipe, two different doughs.

The Convert tool

Every ingredient row has a Convert button. Enter an amount such as 2, 1 1/2 or ¾, choose a unit, and it fills in the grams. Dry goods use King Arthur's weights. Liquids use their real density, so a measuring cup of water is 237 g, milk 244 g, and oil 218 g. Eggs convert at 50 g, and a yolk at 17 g (USDA).

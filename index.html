Understanding Bread Ingredients

Flour

Provides gluten structure - the foundation of bread texture. All-purpose flour has about 10-12% protein, while bread flour is around 12-14%.

Water

Forms gluten networks and activates yeast. Water makes up 60-75% of most breads, with higher hydration resulting in more open textures.

Salt

Controls fermentation speed and enhances flavor. Typically 1.8-2.5% of flour weight, it also strengthens gluten networks.

Yeast

Microorganism that consumes sugar in flour and produces carbon dioxide gas and alcohol, causing dough to rise. Usually 0.1-0.2% of flour weight.

Enrichers (Optional)

Ingredients that add flavor, richness, moisture, and affect hydration.

  • Eggs: Add protein and fat for better structure
  • Milk: Adds sugar for fermentation and moisture
  • Butter: Add fat for tenderness and richer flavor
  • Oils: Provide moisture and make dough more tender
  • Cream: Creates rich, soft textures (contains both water and fat)
